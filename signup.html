NFC Pairing
The glasses have an NFC tag embedded in the right temple. Just tap your phone to the temple — no typing, no scanning. Works with Android (native) and iPhone iOS 14+.
Format: ARG-XXXXX-XXXXX-XX

Where to find your code:
The 15-character code is printed on the inside of the left temple and on the retail box. Format: ARG-XXXXX-XXXXX-XX (letters and numbers). For this demo, enter: ARG-7K3M9-P2Q8R-4X
Point your camera at the QR code on the box or inside the temple
QR Pairing
A QR code is printed on the retail packaging and laser-etched inside the left temple. Point your camera at it to pair instantly. This is the fallback if your phone doesn't have NFC.
