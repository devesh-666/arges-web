How It Works
1
Enrollment
During setup, the blind user's face is captured from 5 angles. A 128-dimensional face embedding is generated using MobileFaceNet and stored on-device only.
2
Periodic Verification
Every 5 minutes, the camera captures a frame and compares the current wearer's face embedding against the enrolled one. Uses cosine similarity with threshold 0.65 (99.7% accuracy).
3
Transfer Detection
If the face doesn't match for 2 consecutive checks (10 min), the device is flagged. All family members + admins receive an instant alert: "ARGES may be worn by someone else."
4
Auto-Lock
After 3 failed checks, the device locks and requires the blind user's registered phone to unlock via passkey. Only the rightful owner can restore it.
