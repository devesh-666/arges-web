/ 04 — Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need internet for ARGES to work?
No — core AI (obstacle detection, OCR, currency, face recognition) runs on-device. The cloud is only used for language translation, family streaming, and the Echo Network.
Is the family video stream private?
Yes — AES-256 end-to-end encryption via LiveKit SFrame. Even ARGES itself cannot decrypt your stream.
How long does the battery last?
8+ hours with the 6000mAh battery. The solar charging strap trickle-charges during outdoor use.
Can the user operate a phone or laptop?
Yes — ARGES OmniAccess provides sight-equivalent control via spoken and haptic feedback.
Does it work in Indian languages?
Yes — 32+ Indian languages via Bhashini, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali.
What if the user falls?
The ADXL345 detects the fall (99.4% accuracy) and triggers Guardian SOS — sending live GPS, audio, and video to family.
